Learning Goals

  • Describe drag as a force that opposes motion through a fluid such as air.
  • Investigate how an object's shape affects drag.
  • Explain how streamlining can reduce drag.
  • Use evidence from an experiment to support a scientific conclusion.
Fair test: When comparing shapes, keep the speed, size, and material the same. Change only one variable at a time.
